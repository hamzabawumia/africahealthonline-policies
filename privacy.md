# Privacy Policy

**AfricaHealthOnline** · Operated by Ican Institute · Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data AfricaHealthOnline collects on **https://africahealthonline.com** and how we
use and protect it. We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Health
information is sensitive, so we collect only what's needed to provide your care.

## 1. What we collect
**Patients**
- **Account details:** name, email address, phone number and password (stored encrypted).
- **Health information:** details you or your doctor record for a consultation, such as symptoms, medical history,
  consultation notes, prescriptions and lab requests.
- **Appointments:** the doctor you booked, and the date and time of each consultation.

**Doctors**
- Name, contact details, professional registration and licence details (which we check with the Medical and Dental Council), practice location, profile photo and biography,
  working hours and fees.

**Everyone**
- **Payment records:** for each payment, the amount, currency, date, status and Paystack reference. **We do not
  receive or store card numbers, mobile money PINs or bank details.** Paystack handles those.
- **Technical data:** standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) and a login session cookie.

## 2. How we use it
- To create and run accounts, and to let patients book doctors.
- To let your doctor hold your consultation, write your prescription and send lab requests.
- To send you your prescription link and appointment reminders.
- To process consultation fees and unlock your prescriptions and lab requests once paid.
- To check that doctors are registered and licensed.
- To answer your questions and keep the platform secure.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use health information for advertising.

## 3. Who can see your health information
- **The doctor you book.** Your doctor can see your details and the records of consultations with them.
- **The laboratory your doctor chooses**, only for the lab requests sent to it.
- **AfricaHealthOnline staff**, only when needed to run the platform or help you, and bound to keep it confidential.

## 4. Who we share data with
- **Paystack**, to process payments. Their privacy policy applies to the details you enter on their checkout.
- **Messaging services** such as WhatsApp or SMS, to send you your private prescription link.

- **Our hosting provider**, which stores the platform's data on our behalf.
- Authorities, only when the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it
- Account data is kept while the account is active.
- Medical records (consultation notes, prescriptions and lab requests) are kept for as long as Ghanaian law and
  medical regulations require, even if an account is closed.
- Payment records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

## 6. Security
Passwords and payment keys are stored encrypted, and the platform uses HTTPS. Prescription links are private and hard
to guess, but please don't forward them. Access to health information is limited to the people listed above. No
system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.
