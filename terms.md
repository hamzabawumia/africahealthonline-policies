## 1. What we provide
AfricaHealthOnline is an online platform that connects patients with registered medical practitioners for
consultations by appointment. Through the platform:
- **patients** can find a doctor, book a consultation, meet the doctor online, and receive e-prescriptions and lab
  requests on their phone;
- **doctors** can run an online clinic, set their hours and consultation fees, write e-prescriptions, send lab requests to the
  laboratories they choose, and keep patient records.

## 2. Not for emergencies
AfricaHealthOnline is **not an emergency service**. If you have a medical emergency, call **112** or go to the nearest
hospital immediately.

## 3. Our role and the doctor's role
- Doctors on AfricaHealthOnline are **independent registered medical practitioners** licensed by the Medical and
  Dental Council of Ghana. **We check every doctor's Medical and Dental Council registration before they can see
  patients.** They are responsible for the medical advice, diagnoses, prescriptions and lab requests they give.
- AfricaHealthOnline provides the technology that makes the consultation, prescription and payment possible. We don't
  give medical advice ourselves.
- A doctor may decide that an online consultation isn't suitable for your condition and advise you to be seen in person.

## 4. Accounts
- **Patients** can browse doctors without an account, but need an account to book a consultation. Please give accurate
  details, including your name, phone number and relevant medical information, so your doctor can care for you safely.
- **Doctors** must provide accurate registration and licence details. We may check them, and we may suspend any account
  whose licence can't be confirmed or is no longer valid.
- Keep your login details private. You're responsible for activity on your account.
- A parent or guardian must book and attend consultations for patients under 18.

## 5. Booking and consultations
- Booking a consultation is **free**.
- Consultations take place online at the booked time. Please be on time, in a private place, with a working internet connection.
- For cancelling or rescheduling, see our [Cancellation Policy](cancellation.md).

## 6. Prices and payment
- Booking is free. You pay for the **consultation**. Each doctor or service provider sets their own consultation fee,
  shown in **Ghana Cedis (GHS)** before you pay.
- You pay the consultation fee after your consultation, through the private link your doctor sends you. Once payment
  is confirmed, you can view and download your prescription and any lab requests from that consultation.
- **All payments are final; we do not offer refunds.** See our [Refund Policy](refund.md) for how we fix payment errors.
- Payments are processed securely by **Paystack** by mobile money or card. We never see or store your card numbers or
  mobile money PIN.
