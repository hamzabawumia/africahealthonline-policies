# Refund Policy

**Ican Apps**, by Ican Institute · Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy covers payments for full-access passes on **https://learn.icanapps.com** (Spelling Bee and Language Learning).

## 1. No refunds
**All payments are final. We do not offer refunds.**

A full-access pass is a **one-time payment** that unlocks all word lists in one app for your account. Because it's
digital content that's available as soon as you pay, please try the free word lists first to make sure the app suits you.

## 2. Payment errors
A payment error isn't a refund, and we'll put it right:
- **Charged more than once** for the same pass: we'll reverse the extra charge.
- **Paid but the pass wasn't unlocked:** we'll unlock it for you. If we can't, we'll reverse the payment.

Report a payment error within **7 days** of paying by emailing **icaninstitute.org@gmail.com** with:
- the email address of your account;
- the **payment reference** (it starts with `SB-` for Spelling Bee or `LL-` for Language Learning, and appears in
  your Paystack receipt); and
- what went wrong.

We reply within 3 working days. Reversed payments go back to the original payment method through Paystack and can take
**5–10 working days** to appear, depending on your bank or network.
