# Refund Policy

**AfricaHealthOnline** · Operated by Ican Institute · Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy covers consultation fees paid on **https://africahealthonline.com**.

## 1. No refunds
**All payments are final. We do not offer refunds.**

Booking a consultation is free. You pay for the consultation, at the fee set by your doctor, shown in Ghana Cedis (GHS)
before you pay. Because the consultation has already taken place and your prescription and lab requests are available
as soon as you pay, please check the patient name, doctor and amount on the payment page before paying.

## 2. Payment errors
A payment error isn't a refund, and we'll put it right:
- **Charged more than once** for the same consultation: we'll reverse the extra charge.
- **Paid but your prescription or lab request didn't open:** we'll unlock it for you. If we can't, we'll reverse the payment.

Report a payment error within **7 days** of paying by emailing **icaninstitute.org@gmail.com** with your name, the phone
number on your account, and the **Paystack payment reference** from your receipt. We reply within 3 working days.
Reversed payments go back to the original payment method through Paystack and can take **5–10 working days** to appear,
depending on your bank or network.

## 3. Questions about your prescription
If you think there's a mistake in your prescription or lab request, contact your doctor or email us, and the doctor
can review and correct it.
