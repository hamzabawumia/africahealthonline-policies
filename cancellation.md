# Cancellation Policy

**AfricaHealthOnline** · Operated by Ican Institute · Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how appointments and accounts on **https://africahealthonline.com** can be cancelled.
AfricaHealthOnline has **no subscriptions**: nothing renews or is charged automatically.

## 1. Cancelling or rescheduling an appointment (patients)
- Booking is free, so there's **no charge** for cancelling or rescheduling.
- Go to **My appointments**, choose the appointment, and cancel it. To reschedule, cancel and book a new time.
- Please cancel at least **2 hours** before the appointment, so the time can be offered to another patient.
  <!-- [CONFIRM: notice period] -->
- If you miss an appointment without cancelling, you can simply book again. Repeated missed appointments may lead to
  booking limits on your account.

## 2. If your doctor cancels
If your doctor cancels or can't attend, we'll let you know by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email, and you can book a new time
with the same doctor or another one. As booking is free, you won't have paid anything for the appointment.

## 3. After a consultation
After your consultation, you pay your doctor's consultation fee through the link your doctor sends you. Payments are
final and can't be cancelled once made. See our [Refund Policy](refund.md).

## 4. Closing your account
- **Patients** can ask us to close their account at any time by emailing us.
- **Doctors** can close their online clinic by emailing us. Please cancel or complete any upcoming appointments first.
  Prescriptions already sent stay available to the patients they were sent to.
- Medical and payment records are kept for as long as the law requires, as explained in our [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
